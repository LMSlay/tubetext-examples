# One source frame, two display moments: a creative handoff example

By TubeText Labs · Static, AI-assisted example · 1 November 2026

An opening frame can reappear after playback. A note that says “the first frame works” leaves a reviewer wondering: **at the start, or as the image left on screen at the end?**

This fictional worksheet separates those two questions. No video, advertisement or Actor was run. The objects, wording and times below are invented; this is not captured output, a rendering test or evidence of campaign performance.

## Why check two moments?

One concrete format illustrates the distinction. The [Ströer Spotlight Video Ad specification](https://specs.stroeer.de/Spotlight%2BVideo%2BAd_ENG.pdf), with a printed date of 3 July 2026 and reviewed on 1 November, describes muted autoplay and a post-playback freeze image that defaults to the video's first frame; another source time can be specified. That is a rule for that particular format, not every feed or video player. Its full-service production offer is not a submission invitation or a TubeText integration.

For any real placement, confirm its current official specification and the actual displayed result. A source video alone does not show what a player leaves on screen.

## Fictional handoff: a cup-lid demonstration

Assume a planned 12-second video. Its opening image shows a cup and detached lid with the text “Which way does this fit?” A later image at source time 00:09 shows the lid attached and the text “See the fitting steps.” For this exercise only, assume the placement returns to the opening image after playback.

| Display moment | Source image used in this invented plan | Visible content in the plan | Interpretation — not a measured result |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: playback begins | Source 00:00 | Cup, detached lid, “Which way does this fit?” | The question could introduce a demonstration. Whether viewers understand or continue is unknown. |
| B: playback has ended | Source 00:00 again, **not source 00:12** | The same detached lid and question; the 00:09 instruction is not in this image. | The remaining image might reopen the question instead of leaving the next step visible. No viewer response has been measured. |

The repeated source time is intentional. **Display state and source time are different fields.** Do not label the post-playback image “the last frame of the video” unless that is what was actually shown. No post-playback duration has been observed here.

## What to hand over

Keep the original asset unchanged. In a separate review note record:

- Asset version and permitted source; placement specification URL and review date.
- Opening image, proposed post-playback image and each source time.
- Planned versus actually observed display state; mark an untested preview **not observed**.
- Visible words/objects separately from your interpretation and unresolved questions.

**Example decision to review, not an instruction to deploy:** compare leaving source 00:00 with leaving source 00:09, if the placement permits that choice. Ask which instruction is visibly present at each moment. Do not claim either choice improves clicks, retention or sales without appropriate evidence.
