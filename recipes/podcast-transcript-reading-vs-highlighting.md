# Podcast transcript available, but not highlighting? Check the source first

By TubeText Labs · Official documentation checked 25 October 2026

A transcript file and a player's reading features are different things. Before paying to generate more text, identify which result you actually need.

## I only want to read or search the episode

Check the show's existing transcript and the player's transcript view first. In Pocket Casts, the episode-details and player views provide transcript access when text is available. A missing toolbar shortcut does not necessarily mean the episode lacks a transcript; consult the [official viewing instructions](https://support.pocketcasts.com/knowledge-base/episode-transcripts/).

## I have a timed transcript. Why does it not follow playback?

Pocket Casts distinguishes transcripts supplied through a show's feed from those it generates itself. Its documentation says synchronized highlighting is reserved for its own generated transcripts, marked with a star. The feature also requires a Plus or Patron subscription, a supported mobile app and an internet connection. Feed-supplied text does not gain that behavior merely by being VTT or SRT; web and desktop reading is not synchronized highlighting.

These are Pocket Casts rules, not a claim about every podcast player. Buying an independent transcription does not by itself change them.

## Does a transcript link mean the publisher checked every word?

No such assurance comes from the tag alone. The [Podcasting 2.0 transcript specification](https://github.com/Podcastindex-org/podcast-namespace/blob/main/docs/tags/transcript.md) links an episode to a transcript or caption file. Multiple formats can be listed. Required URL and media-type fields identify the resource; optional language and caption-related fields describe it. They do not certify human proofreading or grant permission to republish the text.

For research notes, record the episode and transcript sources separately. Label independently generated text as such. Check consequential quotations against the original recording and surrounding context; retain only material you are permitted to use.

## When is independent transcription a different job?

It may be relevant when you need separate speech-to-text output from supported public audio, rather than a player's existing reading feature. [TubeText Podcast Transcript](https://apify.com/tubetext/podcast-transcript) is an optional paid service in that category—not a Pocket Casts highlighting fix, publisher-transcript certification or RSS publishing integration. Review the product's supported inputs and current pricing before deciding.
