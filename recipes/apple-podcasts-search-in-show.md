# Find a phrase in an Apple Podcasts show

If you remember what someone said but not which episode it was, start with **Search in Show**. It looks within one show, so you do not need to transcribe its entire catalog.

1. Open the show in Apple Podcasts and choose **See All Episodes**.
2. Enter a distinctive word, guest name, or short phrase in the search box. Apple says results can use words spoken in an episode transcript as well as the title and guest name. If the phrase gets no result, try a surname, spelling variant, or shorter keyword.
3. Open the matching episode and search its transcript for the term. Tap the matching text to play from that point. For citation or editing, listen to the passage before relying on the wording or time.

See Apple's [Search in Show announcement](https://podcasters.apple.com/support/5612-iOS-27-whats-new-for-apple-podcasts) and [transcript guide](https://podcasters.apple.com/support/5316-transcripts-on-apple-podcasts). Apple describes Search in Show in its iOS 27 release notes; older app versions might not have the same controls.

## If the search misses it

- **The episode is new.** The audio can be playable before Apple finishes generating its transcript.
- **The words were in a dynamically inserted segment.** Apple says changed portions may not appear in the transcript. Search failure does not prove nobody said the words.
- **You need a file for another player or an editor.** Start with a transcript supplied by the publisher. Check it against the exact audio you plan to use, especially after ad breaks. See the [three-anchor spot check](podcast-transcript-audio-version-timestamps.md#a-three-anchor-spot-check).

If the publisher doesn't supply a usable file and you have permission to process the episode, [TubeText Podcast & Video Transcript](https://apify.com/tubetext/any-video-podcast-transcript/input-schema) accepts supported public RSS feeds and direct media links and can produce timestamped text or SRT. Check [the metered pricing](https://apify.com/tubetext/any-video-podcast-transcript/pricing) and limit the request to the episode you need. This creates an off-platform transcript; it does not add any words to Apple Podcasts search. This guide is based on public documentation; no Apple account changes or Actor runs were performed.
