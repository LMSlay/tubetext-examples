# Compare TikTok Shop hooks with product clicks and orders

A public video can show you what the creator did in the opening seconds. It cannot tell you how many orders that opening generated. Use public videos for ideas and your own Seller Center for results.

## Turn one idea into a comparison

1. Pick one product and two openings, such as showing the item in use versus opening with a question. Before recording, write what changes and what stays the same: product, offer, video length, and call to action. Keep paid and organic traffic separate where you can; record differences in audience, distribution, and dates.
2. For your own clips, open **Analytics → LIVE & Video → Video → Videos Performance** in [TikTok Shop US Seller Center](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7464845487671082). Use comparable observation windows. Save the video IDs, product impressions, product clicks and SKU orders. Prefer the dashboard's own definitions and click-to-order metric over a hand-built ratio with a different denominator. Compare product impressions to product clicks, not total video views to product clicks unless you explicitly label that different calculation.
3. Read both stages. More product clicks without more orders may justify inspecting product-page clarity, price, shipping or audience differences. It does not establish why people left.

**Fictional example:** two clips each receive 1,000 product impressions. Clip A gets 80 product clicks and 4 SKU orders; clip B gets 40 product clicks and 4 SKU orders. A generated more recorded product clicks at the same impression count, but it didn't generate more recorded orders in this sample. Four orders each is too little for a confident winner, and this is not a controlled experiment.

## When another tool helps

You can write down the opening, pacing, and call to action manually from a few public references. For a batch of eligible known TikTok URLs, [TubeText Video Breakdown AI](https://apify.com/tubetext/video-breakdown-ai/input-schema) documents hook and visual/script analysis. Use it to label creative approaches, not to fill in conversion numbers. Public cumulative item sales cannot be assigned to a particular competitor video; they mix sources of orders. Check [current Actor pricing](https://apify.com/tubetext/video-breakdown-ai/pricing) before running.
